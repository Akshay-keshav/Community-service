## Live HTTP checks

A read-only GET audit checked **16 distinct destinations** (shared NSP and insurance routes counted once; all three LPG providers counted separately). **13 returned HTTP 200 with HTML**, with titles/final URLs confirming the named portals or the deliberately labelled directory/care page. No account was signed into and no applicant information was sent.

Reached: NSP application login, NHA Beneficiary Portal, Maandhan, PMEGP new application, Bharatgas new connection, PMAY-U application-entry wizard, PMMVY, UDID, Jan Suraksha applicant login, JanSamarth, NCS, NFSA State/UT directory and NHM JSSK care instructions. Jan Suraksha redirects to its canonical `www.jansuraksha.in/login` URL, which is now stored directly.

Limitations:

- **PM-KISAN New Farmer Registration:** the current official indexed form confirms the exact `RegistrationFormupdated.aspx` route. Direct CLI requests failed certificate-chain verification (`UNABLE_TO_VERIFY_LEAF_SIGNATURE`), and web-research requests also encountered fetch/gateway errors. The original official registration link is retained; this is not a claim that the portal does not exist.
- **IndianOil:** the current PMUY application page links to `pages_landingpage`. Its older About-page link redirected to a contact page, so that older URL was not used. A stateless GET exceeded its redirect limit; an anonymous-cookie-session recheck failed local certificate issuer verification. Browser-session availability could not be confirmed. The successfully reached Bharatgas application is listed first, while the original IndianOil and HP alternatives remain available.
- **HP Gas:** the exact New Consumer Registration URL is linked by PMUY. The CLI rejected legacy TLS renegotiation (`ERR_SSL_UNSAFE_LEGACY_RENEGOTIATION_DISABLED`); web research timed out. Browser/security settings were not weakened. PMUY cards also expose Bharatgas and IndianOil links and retain distributor instructions.
- A 200 HTML response establishes that the destination page was reached, not that every post-login application or local intake is available. OTP, identity, lender, document and authority checks remain on the official portals.
- Some web-research requests returned 403 even for pages reached by the independent HTTP check. Route existence was not confused with the research tool’s access limits.

## Status decisions and wording

- Four confirmed-open online applications: Ishan Uday, National Scholarship for Post Graduate Studies, AICTE Pragati degree and AICTE Saksham degree. [NSP](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships) lists closing date **30 November 2026**. OTR/login is required.
- Labour Welfare post-matric student applications closed **31 October 2026**. Later verification dates do not reopen student applications. Its main button still opens the original NSP portal under a neutral label and the closed status remains visible.
- CSSS fresh availability is unconfirmed; renewal announcements are not fresh-application evidence. Its original NSP application portal is directly accessible from the card.
- JSSK is a public-facility route. PMJJBY, PMSBY and MUDRA were corrected from offline-only descriptions to online-or-assisted routes after finding their original portals.
- The remaining 14 application-availability states are not promoted to open merely because a portal exists. Deadline notices are separate: verified year-round Ayushman enrolment says “No fixed application deadline”; sources without a published deadline say “No application deadline listed”; PMMVY displays applicant-specific deadlines. “Application window unconfirmed” is reserved for genuinely uncertain application cycles. Both English and Hindi use these distinctions.

## Interpretation and coverage

- CSSS includes annual income of ₹4.5 lakh; Ishan requires strictly less than ₹4.5 lakh; PG applicants must be under 30 at admission. The original scholarship journey and boundary behavior are preserved. UGC special notes for 2026–27 govern institution recognition.
- Current NSP combination notices and older AICTE financial-support clauses differ. AICTE records show that conflict in both languages and use the stricter PDF condition pending authority confirmation. The labour welfare guideline expressly permits combinations subject to other schemes’ rules.
- PMAY-U matches are preliminary family-level checks. Component-specific loan/value/area limits, ownership and local approval remain in details. MUDRA matching checks business activity; the lender decides credit approval and repayment terms. NCS and UDID are services, not cash awards.
- No state-government records were present in the prototype. All state catalogs await verification. Central records are not a claim of comprehensive state coverage. All 36 State/UT selectors exist, with visible limited-coverage and empty states.
- PMFBY and transitional social-assistance pension revisions were researched but not added without sufficient current implementation/rule verification.
